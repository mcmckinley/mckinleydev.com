Volunteer Work

Netz-AK

Nov 2025 - current

Contributing to the maintenance and development of the network infrastructure for 5,500+ dorms across the state of Baden-Württemberg, Germany.

The Netz-AK (short for Netzwerk Arbeitskreis, or Network Working Group) is a volunteer group responsible for the dormitory network from the sockets in your room to the Internet connection. Netz-AK maintains and develops the network infrastructure to ensure reliable Internet access for all residents. Additionally, the group supports the various student communities of the Dorfrat with their online presence.
